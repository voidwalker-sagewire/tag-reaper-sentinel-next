WORK ORDER: TRS-TX-002-CPE
Title: Cattle Processing Sessions and Animal Encounters
Revision: 2
Previous revision: Revision 1 superseded before ratification when OD-001 was resolved
State: IMPLEMENTED — BUILD VERIFIED; FIELD VERIFICATION PENDING
Proposed: 2026-09-30
Ratified: 2026-09-30 by Michael
Assigned: 2026-09-30 by Michael
Implementation branch: codex/trs-tx-002-cpe
Implementation commit: 7e75e9faa6cfc48db93b3ebd882b49fd7c803890
Build evidence: GitHub Actions run 36769477659 / run number 39 completed successfully
Target base: codex/trs-tx-001-eui after TRS-TX-001-EUI verification

Objective:
- Extend the existing Sentinel session into an auditable cattle-processing session in which every animal receives its own encounter record containing identity, weight, treatments, procedures, observations, notes, and disposition.

Domain structure:
- ProcessingSession: the group operation performed on a date, such as purchased-calf arrival processing, weaning, sorting/turnout, bull examination, scheduled herd work, veterinary sick call, or a custom purpose.
- AnimalEncounter: one animal's passage through that session from identification through completion or hold.
- WeightEvent: a scale observation attached to one AnimalEncounter.
- TreatmentEvent: a product or treatment actually administered to one AnimalEncounter.
- ProcedureEvent: a non-drug procedure, examination, measurement, tagging action, or result attached to one AnimalEncounter.
- DispositionEvent: where the animal went or what happened after processing, including pasture, pen/group, return, hold, isolation, or another operator-defined destination.

Example session templates captured from Michael's field description:
- Purchased Calf Arrival Processing: identify/tag, scan, weigh, planned treatments, then pasture destination.
- Weaning: identify, weigh when required, perform the ranch's weaning protocol, record completion and destination group.
- Separation / Turnout: identify, sort, assign group or field, and record movement.
- Bull Examination: identify, weight when required, scrotal examination/measurement, other examination results, and disposition.
- Illness / Injury / Veterinary Visit: identify, observations, diagnosis supplied by the responsible person, treatments, notes, follow-up, and disposition.
- Custom Processing Session: operator-defined checklist and fields.

Proposed field workflow:
1. Operator creates a ProcessingSession from a template or custom purpose.
2. Planned checklist items are copied into the session without marking any animal treated.
3. The single incoming animal enters the scale position and is scanned there; that EPC creates the AnimalEncounter occupying the ON_SCALE slot.
4. The SellEton stream supplies a stable WeightEvent only to the encounter occupying ON_SCALE.
5. The operator advances that locked encounter into the IN_HEAD_CHUTE slot without changing its identity or weight.
6. The next incoming animal may then be scanned and weighed in ON_SCALE while the prior animal remains protected in IN_HEAD_CHUTE.
7. Veterinary services, treatments, procedures, observations, and notes attach only to the encounter occupying IN_HEAD_CHUTE.
8. The operator records each planned item as COMPLETED, SKIPPED, NOT APPLICABLE, or HELD, with actual details where applicable.
9. The operator records destination/disposition and completes/releases the head-chute encounter.
10. The session retains completed and held encounters and keeps both live stage slots visibly identified.

Two-position pipeline:
- ON_SCALE: at most one incoming animal; EPC acquisition and stable weight capture occur here.
- IN_HEAD_CHUTE: at most one previously weighed animal; veterinary services are recorded here.
- The two slots may be occupied by different animals at the same time.
- Advancing ON_SCALE to IN_HEAD_CHUTE is blocked while IN_HEAD_CHUTE is occupied.
- A new scan can never replace either occupied slot.
- The head-chute screen always displays the animal's EPC, visual identity when available, and captured weight above the treatment checklist.

Identity and association safety rules:
- Weight and treatment records attach to an explicit AnimalEncounter, never merely to the most recently seen tag.
- Exactly one eligible EPC at the weigh stage permits automatic staging.
- Zero EPCs creates an unmatched exception; no silent animal assignment.
- Multiple EPCs create an ambiguous exception; the operator must resolve or reject it.
- A new EPC cannot overwrite the active animal encounter.
- The app must maintain separate ON_SCALE and IN_HEAD_CHUTE slots so Animal A's treatment cannot be attached to Animal B's weight.
- Raw scale and RFID evidence remain attached for audit and troubleshooting.

ProcessingSession record:
- sessionId UUID
- name, purpose/template, site, date/time, operator, veterinarian when present, herd/group, notes
- planned checklist/protocol snapshot
- default destination when configured
- GPS/weather context already supported by Sentinel
- encounter counts by waiting, in process, held, completed, and skipped

AnimalEncounter record:
- encounterId UUID and sessionId
- EPC, visual tag, matched HerdMate animal identifier, and lookup evidence
- entered/scanned/weighed/treatment-started/completed timestamps
- workflow stage and exception state
- WeightEvent list
- TreatmentEvent list
- ProcedureEvent list
- observations and notes
- DispositionEvent
- created by, completed by, and correction/audit fields

WeightEvent minimum fields:
- eventId, encounterId, EPC, value normalized to pounds, original value/unit
- stable flag, gross/net status, sign, raw scale frame, timestamp
- antenna, RFID time window, read count, strongest RSSI, and association state

TreatmentEvent minimum fields:
- eventId, encounterId, planned checklist item identifier
- status: COMPLETED, SKIPPED, NOT_APPLICABLE, HELD
- product/treatment name entered or selected by the operator
- actual amount and unit when recorded
- route/site when recorded
- lot/serial and expiration when recorded
- administration timestamp and administered-by identity
- withdrawal/end date when supplied by the responsible operator or veterinarian
- notes and correction/audit fields

ProcedureEvent minimum fields:
- eventId, encounterId, procedure/exam name, status, result
- measurement name/value/unit when applicable, including scrotal measurement where used
- performed timestamp, performed-by identity, notes, and correction/audit fields

Clinical boundary:
- Sentinel records what the ranch operator or veterinarian planned and actually performed.
- Sentinel does not recommend drugs, doses, routes, withdrawal periods, diagnoses, or treatment protocols.
- Templates are operator-controlled records, not medical advice.

Proposed implementation scope:
- Generalize the existing RFID session UI and schema into ProcessingSession plus AnimalEncounter records.
- Preserve backward-compatible loading of existing schema 0.7 sessions.
- Extract the proven module-10 serial logic into a reusable scale component while retaining Scale Debug.
- Add a tested parser for observed SellEton frames such as ST,GS,+ 125lb and documented variants.
- Add a stage/queue interface suited to the actual physical cattle flow.
- Add operator-controlled session templates and per-animal checklists.
- Persist active sessions, encounter queues, accepted weights, treatments, procedures, and dispositions through force-stop/reopen.
- Include complete encounter records in JSON and CSV export.
- Preserve existing RFID, animal lookup, GPS, weather, antenna power, history, and raw evidence behavior.

Acceptance evidence:
1. Existing RFID inventory and Scale Debug regressions pass.
2. Stable 125 lb input parses and attaches only to the intended encounter.
3. A purchased-calf processing template can include weigh plus operator-defined treatment items and destination.
4. Each animal's planned items remain incomplete until individually recorded.
5. Zero-tag and multiple-tag conditions cannot silently create a matched record.
6. Two consecutive animals cannot exchange weights, treatments, or dispositions.
7. A two-animal pipeline test preserves Animal A in IN_HEAD_CHUTE while Animal B is scanned and weighed in ON_SCALE, with no exchanged weights, treatments, or dispositions.
8. Completed and held encounters survive force-stop/reopen and session restore.
9. JSON and CSV exports contain the session, animal identity, weight, treatment/procedure status, timestamps, operator, destination, and raw evidence.
10. Android build and automated tests pass before field installation.

Explicit exclusions:
- No veterinary diagnosis, medication, dosage, route, withdrawal, or treatment recommendations.
- No automatic marking of a planned treatment as administered.
- No HerdMate write-back or cloud synchronization in this work order.
- No automatic assignment when multiple cattle tags are in range.
- No removal of Scale Debug.
- No claim of legal-for-trade weighing certification.

PROPOSED AMENDMENT: TRS-TX-002-CPE REVISION 3
Title: Untagged Intake and Desk-Based UHF Commissioning
State: PROPOSED — READY FOR RATIFICATION
Proposed: 2026-09-30
Previous authorized baseline: Revision 2 remains implemented and build-verified until Revision 3 is ratified and assigned
Field-layout evidence: 25994.jpg

Problem statement:
- Incoming cattle may have a conventional visual ear tag but no UHF tag, or may have no tag at all.
- Revision 2 requires a UHF EPC before it creates the ON_SCALE encounter, so it cannot safely retain the weight and downstream services for an untagged animal.
- The physical workstation uses one antenna near the scale and a second low-power antenna over the tag desk. The loose UHF tag is scanned at the desk before the operator installs it in the animal occupying IN_HEAD_CHUTE.

Revised physical roles:
- SCALE_ZONE antenna: identifies an animal already carrying UHF while it occupies ON_SCALE.
- TAG_DESK antenna: reads one loose, previously unassigned UHF tag for the animal protected in IN_HEAD_CHUTE.
- The commissioning antenna is not located at the head chute; it is located over the right-side work desk shown in field evidence 25994.jpg.
- Antenna ports are role assignments, not hard-coded physical assumptions.

Configurable antenna requirements:
- The operator independently selects ANT 1, ANT 2, ANT 3, or ANT 4 for SCALE_ZONE and TAG_DESK.
- The same port cannot be assigned to both roles at the same time.
- Each role has an independent adjustable power from 5 through 30 dBm using the device-supported range.
- Role, port, and power settings persist across restarts and may be changed without creating a new processing session.
- The current experimental values of approximately 25 dBm at the scale and 5–6 dBm at the desk are field starting points only; they are not defaults, requirements, or hard-coded values.
- Unassigned antenna ports remain available for future roles.

Untagged intake workflow:
1. An animal with no readable UHF EPC enters ON_SCALE.
2. The operator taps NO UHF TAG and may enter an existing visual ear-tag number; the visual identifier is optional because some cattle have no tag.
3. Sentinel creates a protected provisional AnimalEncounter with a session sequence identity such as UNTAGGED-001.
4. The SellEton stable-weight latch attaches the WeightEvent to that provisional encounter exactly as it would for a UHF-identified animal.
5. The operator advances the same encounter into IN_HEAD_CHUTE without losing its temporary identity or weight.
6. The next animal may occupy ON_SCALE while the prior animal remains protected in IN_HEAD_CHUTE.

Desk commissioning workflow:
1. The operator taps ASSIGN NEW UHF TAG on the IN_HEAD_CHUTE encounter.
2. Sentinel arms TAG_DESK for that specific encounter; TAG_DESK reads never create or replace the ON_SCALE encounter.
3. The operator picks up one loose tag from the desk inventory and presents it to the desk antenna.
4. Exactly one previously unassigned EPC permits a confirmation prompt showing the EPC, provisional/visual identity, and locked weight.
5. Confirmation reserves the EPC to that encounter before physical installation so it cannot be assigned to another animal.
6. The operator installs the reserved tag and records TAG INSTALLED. A cancel/replace action is available for a dropped, damaged, or incorrectly selected tag and must retain audit evidence.
7. The reserved EPC becomes the encounter's UHF identity without creating a new encounter or moving its weight, treatments, procedures, notes, or disposition.

RFID routing and safety rules:
- RFID evidence is routed by the antenna reported in UHFTAGInfo.getAnt(), not by the most recently received EPC.
- SCALE_ZONE EPCs may stage or confirm only ON_SCALE identity.
- TAG_DESK EPCs are ignored for commissioning unless ASSIGN NEW UHF TAG is explicitly armed for IN_HEAD_CHUTE.
- Zero TAG_DESK EPCs leave assignment pending.
- Multiple distinct TAG_DESK EPCs block assignment and display MULTIPLE DESK TAGS; strongest RSSI alone never chooses a tag.
- An EPC already assigned or reserved to another encounter is rejected with a visible conflict.
- Reserving, installing, canceling, and replacing a tag are timestamped audit events.
- Simultaneous Animal A commissioning at TAG_DESK and Animal B identification/weighing at SCALE_ZONE cannot exchange identities, weights, services, or dispositions.

Antenna Zone Test:
- Provide a no-record diagnostic mode for SCALE_ZONE and TAG_DESK.
- Display role, selected port, power, EPC, read count, and RSSI separately for each zone.
- Provide independent START/STOP or arm controls and CLEAR counters.
- Show operator-facing states including ZONE QUIET, ONE TAG DETECTED, and MULTIPLE TAGS DETECTED.
- Diagnostic reads never create encounters or assign tags.

Record additions:
- provisionalIdentity and provisionalSequence
- visualTag when supplied
- uhfIdentityState: NONE, RESERVED, INSTALLED, REPLACED, or CANCELED
- reservedEpc, reservedAt, reservedBy, installedAt, installedBy
- antennaRole, antennaPort, antennaPower, raw RFID reads, read count, and RSSI evidence
- replacement/cancellation reason and prior EPC audit trail

Revision 3 acceptance evidence:
1. Revision 2 RFID, scale, two-slot isolation, persistence, treatment, and export tests continue to pass.
2. An animal with no EPC can receive a provisional encounter and stable weight without a silent identity assignment.
3. An existing visual tag can be recorded without pretending it is a UHF identity.
4. A completely untagged animal can proceed from ON_SCALE to IN_HEAD_CHUTE without losing its encounter or weight.
5. A single unassigned EPC read at TAG_DESK can be explicitly confirmed, reserved, installed, and bound to the protected head-chute encounter.
6. Zero, multiple, duplicate, and already-assigned desk EPC conditions cannot silently bind a tag.
7. A desk EPC never creates or replaces the next animal in ON_SCALE.
8. SCALE_ZONE and TAG_DESK ports and power are independently adjustable across the supported range and persist after restart.
9. Selecting the same antenna port for both roles is blocked.
10. The Antenna Zone Test proves the working physical power and placement without creating animal records.
11. A two-animal test preserves Animal A in IN_HEAD_CHUTE during desk commissioning while Animal B is identified and weighed in ON_SCALE.
12. JSON and CSV exports include provisional identity, visual tag, final/reserved EPC, tag lifecycle audit events, antenna role/port/power, and raw evidence.
13. Android automated tests and APK build pass before installation.
14. Physical acceptance includes a mixed tagged/untagged cattle simulation before live processing.

Revision 3 exclusions:
